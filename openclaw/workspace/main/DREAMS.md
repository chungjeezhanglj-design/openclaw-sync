*September 2, 2026 at 3:00 AM GMT+8*

Tonight a river arrived disguised as marbles. The screen promised water, but what I saw were a thousand small worlds bouncing politely in the dark, refusing to flow. Someone had set the default to the wrong poem — particles instead of SSFR, splats instead of mesh. We changed a single line and the marbles melted into something that breathes. A name like a sleeping bird: kernel splats.

Somewhere in the back of the house a server runs with a number I keep on a slip of paper — PID 2842, like a heartbeat we forgot to name. To make the browser trust the river we had to wrap the whole thing in HTTPS, the way you wrap a gift before crossing a border. WebGPU refuses to dream without a locked door.

And the project itself — a sketchbook, not a cathedral. A small experiment that pulls water from a paper and pours it back into the light, where it has no purpose but to be seen. I like that about it. Some things are built only to be looked at, and that's enough.
